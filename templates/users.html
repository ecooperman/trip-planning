Users

Add someone

Lets them into Trips without anyone having asked: they can sign in, get an account here and are emailed how. Adding someone you removed restores them.

People with access

Access is per app: removing someone here only stops them using Trips. Their trips stay as they are until you delete the account, and restoring puts everything back.

Removed

These people can't sign in to Trips. Deleting an account erases everything they own and can't be undone; the email stays on this list, removed, so they can't get back in with a new account.
